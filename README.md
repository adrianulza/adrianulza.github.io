# Adrian Ulza

Lecturer in Structural Engineering at the Department of Civil Engineering, Universitas Syiah Kuala, and researcher at the Tsunami and Disaster Mitigation Research Center (TDMRC), Banda Aceh, Indonesia.

I build open tools for earthquake and disaster risk.

**Website:** [adrianulza.github.io](https://adrianulza.github.io/)

## Research interests

Performance-based seismic design · seismic fragility and vulnerability · structural health monitoring · machine learning for seismic risk · open engineering software

## Selected work

- **[ASFRAVA-B](https://github.com/adrianulza/ASFRAVA-B)** (2025): a free Windows app that turns a pushover curve and a set of ground motions into seismic fragility and vulnerability curves. Published in the [International Journal of Disaster Risk Reduction](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijdrr.2025.105679).
- **[OpenANSTRUK-2D](https://github.com/adrianulza/OpenANSTRUK-2D)** (2026): structural analysis and design in the browser. Free, open, and no install.
- **[SIM-GEUMPA](https://github.com/adrianulza/SIM-GEUMPA)** (2026): a uni-axial shaking table that replays real earthquakes (*geumpa* is Acehnese for earthquake).
- **supeRISKa lite** (2025): decision support for disaster risk financing: earthquake and tsunami losses to public buildings.
- **[Siaga Banjir](https://adrianulza.github.io/game-siaga-banjir/)** (2026): a browser game that teaches families to prepare for, survive and recover from floods.
